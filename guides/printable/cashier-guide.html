What to do

1

Read the pump label

Match Pump number and nozzle: Premium, Regular, Regular 2, or Diesel.

2

Leave Previous Closing alone

Gray fields come from the prior confirmed shift. Cashier does not edit them.

3

Tap Current Closing

The faint zero is only a placeholder. Type the whole register number yourself.

4

Keep the decimal

If the physical register says 409019.92, type 409019.92 - not 409019 and not 40919.92.

5

Check liters sold

The app calculates liters. If it looks impossible, recheck the copied digits before continuing.

Important rules

No sales / closed stationCurrent Closing may be exactly the same as Previous Closing. That correctly produces 0 liters sold.
Never invent a readingCopy what the physical pump shows. If it appears lower or unusually high, recheck every digit; do not change it just to remove a warning.
Do not switch productsPremium entered under Regular can create very large sales and variance.
Typing problem?Click once inside the box, press Ctrl+A or Select All, then type the full number. Do not use the mouse wheel to change values.
Quick check409019.92 - 408967.16 = 52.76 liters
Before Next: Every configured pump/nozzle is checked, decimals are present, and no Premium/Regular/Diesel row is switched.
