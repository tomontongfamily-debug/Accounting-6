Correct procedure

1

Choose the affected product

Premium, Regular, or Diesel.

2

Enter the exact effective time

Use the time the pump selling price officially changed.

3

Enter the new selling price

Do not overwrite the old part of the shift.

4

Get every matching nozzle reading

Enter the physical register reading at the change time for all nozzles selling that product.

5

Confirm once

The system divides sales before and after the change reading.

Do not do these

Do not use final closingThe change reading is the reading at the exact price-change time, not the shift-end reading.
Do not skip a nozzleEvery nozzle selling the affected product needs a change reading.
Do not use this for tomorrowA price effective before the next shift begins belongs in normal Fuel Price Setup.
ExampleRegular changed from ₱75.80 to ₱76.30 at 1:30 PM. Sales before register 325950.00 use ₱75.80; sales after it use ₱76.30.
Before confirming: product, effective time, new price, and every matching pump/nozzle reading are complete.

Meaning of each field

Deposit DateThe day the bank accepted the money.
Sales Date CoveredThe sales day that produced the cash.
CoversShift 1, Shift 2, Shift 3, a combination, or All Shifts.
BankThe receiving bank.
ReferenceBank reference; Liloan may use Minutes and Seconds.
AmountThe exact amount of this one bank transaction.

Coverage rules

One deposit covers many shiftsEnter it once and select the combined coverage. Never repeat the full amount under every shift.
One deposit covers two sales datesSplit it into separate entries by sales date and correct covered shifts. The split totals must equal the real bank transaction.
Not deposited yetDo not create a fake deposit. The amount remains pending cash on hand until the real deposit is entered.
Admin verificationSaving creates a Pending deposit. Only admin verifies it against bank records.

Common example

1Yesterday Shift 2 + 3Select yesterday as Sales Date
2Deposited todaySelect today as Deposit Date
3One combined amountSave once with Shift 2 + Shift 3
