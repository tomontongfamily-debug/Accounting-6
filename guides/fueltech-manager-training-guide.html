Daily pricing

Use this for normal days. The price applies to the whole day and continues until a new price is saved.

Shift pricing

Use this only when one shift has a different price. Choose the exact shift before confirming.

Important: Do not leave prices blank when there are fuel sales. Cashier may be blocked or sales may be wrong.
If cashier sees "pump price missing": enter the correct fuel price for that sales date/shift, press Confirm Fuel Prices, then ask cashier to refresh and submit again.

4. Price Change During Shift

Use this only when the price changes in the middle of an active shift. This section teaches the "before and after price/register" rule.

Correct

  • Old price: before effective time.
  • New price: after effective time.
  • Price-change register is the pump reading at the exact change time.
  • Enter every matching nozzle for that product.

Wrong

  • Do not overwrite whole-day price if change happened mid-shift.
  • Do not leave one nozzle blank.
  • Do not type a price-change reading lower than opening or higher than closing.
Simple example: Regular price changed from 71.50 to 72.00 at 1:30 PM. The pump register at 1:30 PM was 325950.00. Sales before 325950.00 use 71.50. Sales after 325950.00 use 72.00.
Ask cashier for the exact pump register at the price-change time. Do not use the final closing reading as the price-change reading.

Combined deposit rule: If one bank deposit covers many shifts on the same sales date, enter it once with the correct coverage. Do not enter the same amount three times.
If one real bank transaction covers two sales dates: split it by sales date so reports count correctly. Example: one entry for yesterday Shift 2 + Shift 3, and one entry for today Shift 1. Use the same bank/reference if needed, but do not combine two sales dates into one row.

9. Request Change Or Removal

Request Change

Use when the deposit is real but some detail is wrong: amount, bank, reference, date, or coverage.

Request Removal

Use when the deposit should not exist: duplicate entry, wrong station, or completely wrong sales date.

Do not secretly fix deposit history. Press Request Change or Request Removal and wait for admin approval.
