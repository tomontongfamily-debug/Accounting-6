First do the opening setup

Complete and submit October 2 Shift 3 in the existing cashier app. This shift runs from October 2 at 10 p.m. to October 3 at 4 a.m. Its final pump readings and tank inventory automatically become the new opening balances.

The new system waits until 4 a.m. and until those readings are complete. Check the opening numbers against the station readings before using the first new report. Old reports stay saved. Keep old undeposited cash separately identifiable and finish its deposits in the old workflow.

Cashier each shift

  1. On your phone: open Cashier, choose Liloan and the correct shift. Photograph every required pump/nozzle reading. Check the number shown, correct any misread digit, then confirm. All required readings must be completed.
  2. On the PC: open the same cashier link and shift. Confirm the phone readings have arrived. Enter the actual tank readings, fuel deliveries, oil sales and any other applicable manual entries.
  3. Count the physical cash: enter the number of ₱1,000, ₱500, ₱200, ₱100, ₱50, ₱20, ₱10, ₱5 and ₱1 pieces. Confirm the count and complete cash reconciliation. Recount when the app asks.
  4. Submit the report once: check that it shows submitted. Missing photos, readings or cash checks must be completed before submission. Tell the manager about any remaining error.

What comes in automatically

Online Pay, posted PO transactions and eligible station cash CVs come from their source apps. Do not enter the same deduction again.

Approved, liquidated and cleared CVs count once. Pending, declined and cancelled CVs are not deducted. Cancelling a CV removes its deduction; a recorded physical cash count is kept, so a difference may become visible. The system keeps a record of the change.

POS redemption is not connected yet. Check and enter applicable redemption amounts through the existing manual fields.

Manager each shift

  1. Check fuel prices and opening readings. Review the reasons shown for unusual reports.
  2. For a price change during a shift, enter the product, new price and actual change time. Use the manager link on a phone to photograph and confirm every affected nozzle. Check and confirm the change on the PC.
  3. For a bank deposit, select one shift card or several shift cards. Enter the actual deposit date, bank, amount and reference, then send it for bank verification. Explain partial deposits or actual approved adjustments. A shift already included in a deposit cannot be selected twice.

If something looks wrong

Check the station, date, shift and internet connection first. Phone readings normally appear on another visible screen within about 15 seconds; source deductions refresh within about a minute. Retry a failed save and verify the result. Do not replace a missing import with a guessed amount. Ask the manager; Admin can see Error Analytics, Station Health reasons and the audit record. If there is no connection, keep the readings and supporting details for entry when service returns.

Phone photos still need a person to verify the displayed reading. Use the first shifts to compare the app with actual pump readings, cash and source references. Print this guide using your browser's Print command.
