2

Choose the pump. Tap Open camera or Take / choose photo. Photograph the reading clearly.

Check that all nozzles show complete. Missing photos or readings block the report.

PHONE → SAVED → PC

On the PC, open the same shift and verify the confirmed readings have arrived. It normally refreshes within about 15 seconds.

Before the first new shift

Start: October 3, 2026 at 4:00 a.m. Manila time. First submit October 2 Shift 3 in the existing cashier app. Its closing pump readings and tank inventory become the new opening balances.

The new workflow waits for the start time and complete readings. Old reports stay saved. Keep old undeposited cash identifiable and finish those deposits in the old workflow.

02

Count, review, submit

The phone handles pump photos. Finish the shift report on the PC.

Actual cash-count screen • All quantities and amounts are examples.

1

Count the notes and coins

In Physical Cash, enter the number of pieces for each denomination, including ₱200. The app calculates the total.

2

Confirm and continue

Click Confirm physical cash, then Save and Next. Verify the phone pump readings in Pump Register.

3

Complete the remaining entries

Review imported payments and deductions. Enter tank dips, deliveries, oil sales, Coke ending count and applicable manual redemption or other entries.

In the final Review and Submit step

4. Check your cash count

Click Check my cash count. It uses your earlier confirmed total. Enter a new denomination count only if a recount is requested.

5. Submit once

Check your cashier name and readings, then click Submit Shift Report. Verify the shift card shows Submitted. Resolve missing requirements first.

03

Deposit by shift card

One shift or several shifts can be included in one bank deposit.

1

Select the shift cards

Check the deposit date. Tick the submitted shifts to include, then click Create deposit. Use Select all shifts for a batch.

2

Copy the details from the deposit slip

Enter the bank, reference number and actual amount deposited. Add any applicable approved expense or partial-deposit reason.

3. Review → send for verification

Click Review deposit, check the details, then Submit for bank verification. Look for it under Submitted deposits.

Pending or verified deposits already reserve their shifts. Do not submit the same cash again. If the amount is short or partial, record the actual amount and explain it.

When a fuel price changes

Record the actual change time and photograph every affected nozzle.

Cropped phone screens • Example time and price only.

1

Open Manager on your phone

Select Liloan and the correct report date and shift. Open Price Change During Shift and add a change if needed.

2

Set product, time and new price

Use the actual time the price changes. Do not replace the price for the whole shift.

3

Photograph at the change time

Take photos of all affected nozzles. Check the detected numbers and confirm each reading.

4

Check the change on the PC

Open the same report on the manager PC. Verify the photos and readings arrived, then confirm the mid-shift price change.

If anything looks wrong

Check the station, date, shift and connection. Verify a failed save before retrying. Keep evidence if offline; never guess a missing imported amount. Ask Admin to review Station Health reasons and Error Analytics.

Automatic imports: do not enter them twice

CV + Online Pay + PO

Eligible source transactions are matched to the station and shift. Approved, liquidated and cleared cash CVs count once.

Excluded / still manual

Pending, declined and cancelled CVs are excluded. POS redemption is still manual until its separate app is connected.

Shift 1

4:00 a.m. to before 1:00 p.m.

Shift 2

1:00 p.m. to before 10:00 p.m.

Shift 3

10:00 p.m. to before 4:00 a.m. next day

1:00 p.m. and 1:01 p.m. belong to Shift 2. After midnight but before 4 a.m. belongs to the previous date’s Shift 3. All times are Philippine time.

Source timing: Online Pay uses the payment time, PO the transaction time, and CV the original approval time. Imported deductions normally refresh within about a minute.

Cancelled CVs: cancelling removes the deduction and keeps the recorded physical cash count, so a cash difference may become visible. The system keeps an audit record.

First-shift check: compare the app with actual pump readings, cash and source references. A person still needs to verify each photographed reading.
